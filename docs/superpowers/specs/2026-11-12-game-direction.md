# Game direction: PvE war, real logistics, cooperation

## Reform (2026-11-12, evening): the player is a battalion commander

This replaces the "player is the general of a whole army" framing; the premise and the threat below stay.

- **You are one commander** in humanity's imperium, deployed in the field. You command your **battalion**,
  thousands of troops as one force, with F.E.A.R.-style GOAP AI below you.
- **Budget: about 1,000 units per player for now.** Every soldier can be a full agent with real AI, at full detail.
- **You build your battalion:** its composition, its structure and its specialism. Some battalions are diplomatic,
  some assault, some defence, some balanced.
- **Forward operating bases:** commanders build them on the front.
- **A huge multiplayer PvE world:** players together reclaim the universe and make contact with other
  civilisations. Cooperation at large scale is the point.
- **Later:** players request resources, assaults and reinforcements from one another.
- **Where you appear:** each player starts on the home planet as a single commander. Once players create,
  capture or rebuild cities, other players may spawn there too.
- **You manage, you do not puppet.** Troops join your battalion; you run it. You feed them, tend to their needs and
  keep their morale up. Soldiers who are rested, well fed and free of combat fatigue fight better, and can even be
  eager for a fight.
- **Permadeath.** Soldiers can be severely wounded. If you have the equipment, you rescue them and bring them back
  to the ship to be treated. Depending on the wounds, they may need transport to a field hospital, unless the ship
  carries the equipment to treat them on board.
- **Light infantry look:** an olive jumpsuit with chest pockets, a dark hood and mask, dark shoulder and elbow pads,
  one rolled sleeve, a pouch belt, a drop-leg holster and a rear back pouch.


Status: direction decided 2026-11-12. Lore and system details are proposals, marked as such.

## Decisions from the project lead

- **The game is mostly about war, and logistics is just as central.** Logistics is **fully physical**: every shell, fuel can and ration is produced, loaded and moved by trucks, trains and ships that you can watch, and that the enemy can cut off. Without supply, units stop fighting.
- **The shared galaxy is PvE.** Everyone fights a **spreading AI threat**, in the spirit of Helldivers 2, together with a growing coalition of species. See the threat and coalition section below.
- **Defeating the threat matters more than any PvP.** The game does **not** reward PvP in the galaxy. It rewards **cooperation**, and working together moves the whole galaxy forward.
- **Persistence:** imperiums, territory, infrastructure and veterans persist.
- **The galactic mode has four long-term goals, all of them:**
  1. Push the threat back, sector by sector.
  2. Sustain the frontier: supply lines that keep territory and production alive.
  3. Build megaprojects together through real logistics.
  4. Personal legacy: ranks, medals, veteran units, and a war record.
- **PvP is a separate mode** on smaller maps, where players start with a set amount of technology already researched. It does not affect the galaxy.

## Time scale (decided 2026-11-12)

| What | Real time |
|---|---|
| A game day: the planet's spin, day and night | 60 minutes |
| A home planet's orbit around its sun | 1 day (outer planets proportionally longer; Brine takes 3 days) |
| Crossing the galaxy | 1 week |

## The threat and the coalition (decided 2026-11-12, details open)

- **Origin:** we built an AI and abused it, and it turned against us. It then built a superintelligence of its own, which now pushes against every civilisation it finds. We lost our grip on our own technology because the machines were ours. The inspiration is The Matrix, with the story and names kept original.
- **Not only humanity:** other species are also threatened, and none of them can beat it alone. They have to unite, the way Mass Effect brings its races together.
- **The galactic arc:**
  - Players start as humanity.
  - Together, the player base must make contact with the other species and bring them into the coalition. Their names are still open.
  - Each species that joins brings its worlds, its technology and its ways of fighting into the shared war.
  - First contact and each species joining are the big collective events of the galaxy, driven by "major orders".
- **The technology dilemma still applies:** recovering old technology is how the galaxy progresses, but powerful old machines attract the enemy, or can be turned by it.
- **Open:** whether the AI can ever be bargained with, or only beaten. The abuse gives it a motive, and that could make a strong late-game moral choice for the whole galaxy.

## Proposal: rewarding cooperation, not PvP (to design)

- **Merit, not kills.** Players earn merit for things that help the war:
  - holding the front;
  - delivering supplies to *other players'* fronts;
  - escorting convoys;
  - contributing to megaprojects;
  - taking part in major orders.
- **Shared outcomes pay everyone.** When a major order or a sector liberation succeeds, every contributor is rewarded in proportion to their contribution, never to their kills.
- **Supply contracts.** A player can post a need ("200 t of shells to Brine front"). Whoever fulfils it earns merit and the requester's gratitude, which works like a reputation.
- **No gain from hurting players.** Destroying, blocking or raiding another player's units or supply lines earns nothing in the galaxy. Imperium borders change only through administration and logistics, never through force between players.
- **Legacy:** medals, a war record and veteran units are tied to merit and to the sectors you helped hold, and carry across seasons.

## What this changes in the current plan

- Navigation and the testing grounds are unchanged; logistics needs them, because trucks and trains drive the same nav grid.
- The AI decisions (CoH-style cover, player as general) apply to both the player's forces and the threat's forces.
- New design items, after navigation:
  1. A physical logistics model: producers, depots, cargo items, transport and consumption.
  2. The threat's spread model on the galaxy map.
  3. The merit and cooperation system.
  4. The PvP mode with fixed starting technology.
